# Who we are

I-way is a logistics platform that helps businesses plan shipments, stay informed about progress, and keep deliveries organized in one place. We are currently opening through early access.

**Brand line:** Move Smarter. Go Further.  **Descriptor:** Logistics made simpler.

**Personality:** effortless · trustworthy · forward-moving · approachable.

# How we sound

- Short sentences, everyday words, one idea at a time.
- Describe what customers can do, never how the technology works.
- Promise only what is verified. No invented statistics, customers, testimonials, certifications, guarantees or prices.
- Say plainly what is available now and what is not.
- Calm, specific error messages. No exclamation marks in product UI.

# How we look

| Element | Rule |
|---|---|
| Logo | Use the supplied SVG unaltered. Clear space = ¼ of logo height. Minimum 96 px wide. On Navy or photos, place it on a white chip. |
| Colour | Navy #092448 · Blue #1261F3 · Cyan #00BCE8 · Orange #FF681C · Amber #FFB000 · Cloud #F5F8FC · Ink #111827 |
| Orange | One primary action per view. **Navy text on Orange** (white fails contrast at 2.9:1). Never orange body text. |
| Type | Inter (400–800). Headlines extra-bold, sentence case, tight tracking. Tabular figures for numbers. |
| Shape | Cards 20 px radius, buttons 12 px, generous whitespace, soft shadows. |
| Icons | One outline family: 24 px grid, 1.75 px stroke, rounded, single colour. |
| Imagery | Original illustrations and honest product previews labelled "illustrative". No generic AI imagery. No third-party logos. |
| Status | Always colour **and** word. Blue in progress · Green done · Amber attention · Red problem. |
| Motion | Subtle; stops under reduced-motion. |

# Ten rules

1. Show the outcome, not the mechanism.
2. Never put strategy, roadmap, AI or agent mechanics, architecture, algorithms, settlement rules, margins or Platform Admin on a public surface.
3. Never use "AI-native", "AI-powered" or AI imagery as public headlines.
4. Never show fabricated social proof, partner logos or performance numbers.
5. Never alter, recolour, invert, stretch or re-typeset the logo.
6. Never use white text on Orange, or Orange, Cyan or Amber as text on light backgrounds.
7. One Orange call-to-action per view.
8. Label all sample data "illustrative".
9. Be honest about early access; no false scarcity.
10. Every public asset passes the IP-disclosure review and `npm run verify`.

# Instructions for AI assistants and agents

Use only the tokens in `docs/brand/tokens/`. Do not recreate the logo from a screenshot or concept board. Treat `i-way-brand-and-landing-preview.png` as mood reference only; do not copy its statistics, partner logos or AI claims. When unsure whether something is public-safe, leave it out and flag it.
