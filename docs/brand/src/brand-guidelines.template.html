01 · Foundation

Brand foundation

Purpose

Make moving goods simpler, so businesses and the people who deliver for them spend less time coordinating and more time moving forward.

Promise

One connected experience: plan the work, see what is moving, keep everything organized.

Brand line

Move Smarter. Go Further.

Always sentence case with full stops, always as two sentences. Descriptor: “Logistics made simpler.”

Positioning (public)

A logistics platform that helps businesses plan shipments, stay informed and keep deliveries organized. Say what customers can do, never how the technology works.

Personality: four pillars

Effortless

Few steps, plain words, no clutter.

Trustworthy

Verified claims, visible status, honest limits.

Forward-moving

Direction, momentum, progress, never hype.

Approachable

Professional, human, never stiff.

Audiences

AudienceWhat they need from the brandWhere they meet it
Businesses that ship goodsClarity, confidence, less chasingPublic website, notifications, tracking links
Carriers, dispatchers, accountingControl, accuracy, speedCarrier Admin Portal
DriversGlanceable, one-handed, no ambiguityDriver Mobile App
Fleet companiesOwnership of their fleet and payFleet Company Portal
I-way staffGoverned visibilityPlatform Admin (internal)
Brand note on “AI”. The product is described internally as AI-native. Publicly, I-way does not lead with AI, show AI mechanics or use AI-themed imagery. The directive asks for outcomes, not mechanisms, and warns against generic AI imagery and buzzwords.

Clear space and size

Rules

  • Clear space X = ¼ of the logo height on every side. Nothing enters this zone.
  • Minimum size: logo 96 px wide on screen (25 mm in print); icon 24 px (16 px for favicons only).
  • Placement: top-left in headers; centred only on single-focus pages.
  • Icon alone for app tiles, favicons, avatars. Never use the icon where the wordmark is needed for recognition (first impressions, press).

Rules

  • Headlines: tight tracking (−0.02em), extra-bold, Navy. Sentence case, never all caps except eyebrows.
  • Body line length 45–75 characters. Left-aligned. Avoid justified text.
  • Numbers in tables, settlements and invoices use tabular figures (font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums) and right alignment.
  • Product UI: 14–16 px body in dense portals; 18 px minimum body in the Driver App, primary button label 18–20 px bold.
  • The supplied logo wordmark is Arial-based artwork; it is not typeset in Inter. Never retype it.

06 · Layout, shape, icons, imagery, motion

Layout, shape, icons, imagery and motion

Shape

Cards 20 px radius · buttons 12 px · fields 10 px · chips fully rounded. Soft, friendly, never sharp.

Spacing (4-pt grid)

4 · 8 · 12 · 16 · 24 · 32 · 48 · 72 · 104. Generous whitespace between sections (64–104 px), tight inside components.

Elevation

Shadow 1 0 8px 24px rgba(9,36,72,.06) for cards. Shadow 2 0 24px 60px rgba(9,36,72,.14) for hero and floating elements. No hard black shadows.

Iconography

{{ICONS}}

One family: 24 px grid, 1.75 px stroke, round caps and joins, single colour (currentColor), shown in a 44–52 px tile (radius 12–14) with a soft tint of its colour. Do not mix filled and outline icons, emoji or third-party icon styles in one interface.

Imagery and illustration

Do

  • Original, clean logistics illustrations in brand colours
  • Interface previews with sample data labelled “illustrative”
  • Authentic photography only if owned or licensed, showing real work, people and vehicles without other brands
  • Route lines, pins, parcels, trucks as simple geometric forms

Don’t

  • Generic “AI” imagery: brains, circuits, robots, glowing networks
  • Third-party brand logos or liveries (carriers, retailers, ports)
  • Crowded dashboards or technical diagrams on public pages
  • Stock images that imply customers, scale or coverage we cannot verify

Motion

Subtle and purposeful: 150–200 ms for interactions, up to 600 ms for gentle reveals, route-line dash and a slow float on hero cards. Everything stops under prefers-reduced-motion. No autoplay video, no parallax, no animation that carries essential information.

Data visualisation

Primary series Blue; secondary Cyan; highlight Orange (one series); warnings Amber; neutrals from Navy tints. Always label directly or in a legend with text; never rely on colour alone.

Forms and feedback

  • Visible label above every field; optional fields marked “(optional)”; required kept to a minimum.
  • Errors sit under the field in red text with a clear fix; inputs persist after errors; focus moves to the first error.
  • Loading states say what is happening (“Sending…”). Success states say what happens next.
  • Focus ring: 3 px Blue, 3 px offset, on every interactive element.

09 · Public vs. private

Public vs. private brand rules

Brand expression must never leak how I-way works. Show the customer-facing outcome, not the proprietary mechanism.

Public (website, social, press)Private (documents, apps, repositories)
What I-way helps customers doHow the technology accomplishes it
Simple shipment experienceInternal shipment orchestration
Tracking and visibilityData processing and intelligence models
Customer benefitsProprietary algorithms
Easy registrationIdentity and permission architecture
Clear pricing, when approvedPricing algorithms and margin models
Customer trustInternal security architecture
  • Never in public brand material: strategy, roadmap, agent or AI mechanics, architecture, algorithms, settlement rules, margins, ownership model, Platform Admin, internal metrics.
  • Brand assets in the public repository carry no internal names, comments or metadata.
  • Every public asset passes the IP-disclosure review before release.
