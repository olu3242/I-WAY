# Implemented contracts and certification limits

This is internal application documentation. No hosted target, identity or access grant is activated by these contracts.

Load drafts are server-side tenant resources. Saving incomplete work never approves a shipment. Advancing validates the completed stage; submission validates the entire contract and creates one load/shipment transaction. A reviewed correction is allowed only before assignment, requires an expected version and reason, synchronizes the shipment and resets validation. Execution and approved financial sources cannot be corrected through this endpoint. Appointments explicitly use UTC; automatic facility-zone input conversion remains incomplete. Carrier-private notes and billing terms are removed from driver/fleet projections.

An expense belongs to the assigned driver and the employing company recorded on the load. A carrier reviews carrier-driver expenses; a fleet reviews its own fleet-driver expenses. Approved expenses are immutable. A submitted or approved receipt reference cannot be reused for the same driver/load, with case and surrounding whitespace normalized; a withdrawn/rejected submission can be resubmitted for review. Idempotency protects lost responses and duplicate clicks. Receipt references are not receipt images and do not establish that an expense is genuine without human review.

Carrier-driver reimbursements enter that driver's settlement. Fleet-driver reimbursements enter fleet-owned driver pay, within the approved fleet settlement budget. Drafts capture expense IDs and versions. New approvals make an older draft stale until recalculation/revision. A source load belongs to one payee/week statement, preventing the same approved expense from entering multiple approved weekly statements through these workflows. There is no actual payment transfer.

Fleet compensation always requires explicit terms and a reason/reference. Percentage uses summed completed driver source-load revenue and explicit half-up cent rounding. Mileage uses integer paid miles entered against every completed source load with a verification reference; it is not measured GPS distance. Per-load, flat weekly, weekly salary and positive custom agreed lines use integer cents. These contracts do not implement wage compliance, proration or legal payroll deductions. `model` and `terms` are authoritative for revised driver pay; the legacy top-level per-load field is retained for creation compatibility and must not be used to interpret other models. Employment, source approval/version and aggregate fleet budget are rechecked on approval.

Onboarding records support non-sensitive profile preparation, submission, review, information recovery/rejection and activation. A pending record imposes a dispatch hold. Carrier operating approval and verified compliance remain separate conditions. The workflow never creates identities, grants roles or approves sensitive documents merely because a profile is complete. Verification references identify an owner-approved external verification process; they are not uploaded document evidence. Real invitation delivery, phone/email verification and sensitive storage remain blocked by the recorded ownership/security decisions.

Platform cases live only in the governance namespace. Explicit platform case/config/support permissions and a trusted AAL2 session are required. The PRD source/category/status catalog is server-owned. The support response setting applies to subsequently created cases and cannot change authentication, deployment, billing or tenant permissions. Support requests contain a reason, target, bounded scope and expiry; their status is awaiting owner approval. No cross-tenant reader or access-grant adapter is configured. Normal platform dispatch remains denied.

PDF generation rechecks current principal permissions and source status on every download. Approved statements become unavailable after reopening. Shipping summaries are explicitly distinct from original rate confirmations and signed BOL/POD. Downloads use server UUID filenames, private no-store responses and audit hashes, with no raw financial content in the download audit. PDFs are regenerated from authorized sources; original-upload retention is not implemented. Inter 4.1 is embedded without modification under the retained OFL license. Supported Unicode is preserved; a missing glyph fails with `PDF_GLYPH` instead of substitution. PDF/UA and human assistive-technology conformance are not certified.

Functional evidence uses synthetic identities, disposable SQLite and genuine restricted PostgreSQL 17.11 logins. Mock Google/password/MFA flows do not certify hosted providers. Original Figma exports/version remain unavailable. The complete 72-row gap register distinguishes remaining implementation from owner/approval/reference blockers. A01–A12 retain their historical identifiers and superseding target exclusions.
