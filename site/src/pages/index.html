FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What is I-way?

I-way is a logistics platform that helps businesses plan shipments, stay informed about progress, and keep deliveries organized in one place. It is currently opening through early access.

Who can use I-way?

I-way is for businesses that ship goods and the trucking teams that move them. Drivers, carriers and fleet companies can reach their sign-in options from the Sign In page.

How do I get started?

Select Get Started and share a few details. We will follow up with next steps as access opens for your needs.

Can I track my shipments?

Following shipment progress is central to the I-way experience. What is included for your account will be confirmed with you during early access.

Does I-way support business deliveries?

Yes. I-way is designed for business deliveries and freight. Tell us what you move on the early access form so we can share relevant next steps.

How much does I-way cost?

Pricing has not been published yet. We will share pricing details once they are available.

Can I use I-way on my phone?

This website works on phones, tablets and computers. We will share details about mobile availability once they are confirmed.

How can I contact support?

Visit the Contact page and send us a message. We will reply by email.
